How Extractive Text Summarization and Sentence Scoring Work

Modern client-side summarizers utilize extractive text algorithms. Instead of generating brand new sentences (abstractive AI), extractive models evaluate every sentence in the source text based on several linguistic criteria:

1. Word Frequency & Keyword Density: Sentences containing recurring high-frequency terms receive higher importance scores.

2. Sentence Position: Introductory and concluding sentences in paragraphs typically carry the primary thesis of the text.

3. Sentence Length Penalty: Extremely short filler phrases or overly convoluted sentences are deprioritized to ensure smooth readability.

Furthermore, I integrated an exclusive Live Compression Ratio Auditor that monitors word count reduction in real time, giving users immediate feedback on efficiency and reading time savings.

The Mathematical Logic of Text Condensation & Compression Ratios

To evaluate summary efficiency and word reduction metrics during editorial audits, I utilize standard mathematical models:

Core Summarization & Compression Formulas: 1. Word Reduction Percentage = ((Original Word Count - Summary Word Count) / Original Word Count) * 100 2.
