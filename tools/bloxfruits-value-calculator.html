Understanding Fruit Demand vs. Value

In Blox Fruits, Value is a numerical score assigned to each fruit based on rarity and usefulness in grinding or PvP. However, Demand dictates how quickly someone will accept that fruit. For example, a fruit with high demand can often command a slight overpay because everyone wants it.

Top Trading Rules to Remember

To ensure you never get scammed or take a bad loss, keep these golden rules in mind:

1. Value Difference Limit: Remember that Blox Fruits has an in-game trade value restriction where the value difference between offered items cannot exceed 40%.

2. Prioritize Demand: Always value utility fruits like Buddha and Portal higher than their raw numerical score because their PvP and grinding demand is unmatched.

3. Use This Calculator: Whenever someone rushes you into a trade in-game, quickly open this tool on your phone or PC to verify values.
