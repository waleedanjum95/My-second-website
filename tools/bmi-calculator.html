What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a commonly used screening measurement that compares a person's weight with their height. It can provide a simple indication of whether a person's weight falls within a general range considered underweight, healthy, overweight, or obese for adults.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, if a person weighs 70 kg and has a height of 1.75 meters, the BMI is calculated by dividing 70 by 1.75 squared. The result is approximately 22.9.

Adult BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30.0 or higher

These categories are general screening ranges for adults. BMI does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately represent body composition for athletes, highly muscular people, older adults, or certain other groups.

Why Use a BMI Calculator?

BMI can be useful as a quick reference when monitoring changes in body weight. It can help people understand whether their current weight falls within a commonly used adult BMI range. However, BMI should not be treated as a complete measure of health.

Waist measurement, body composition, physical activity, diet, medical history, and other health factors can also be important. If you have concerns about your weight or health, consider discussing your individual situation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or distinguish muscle from fat.

What BMI is considered healthy for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range for adults.
