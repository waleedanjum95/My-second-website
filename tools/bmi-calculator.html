What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index, commonly called BMI, is a screening measurement that compares a person's weight with their height. It is widely used for adults as a simple way to identify general weight categories.

Our free BMI Calculator makes the calculation quick and convenient. Enter your height in centimeters and weight in kilograms to instantly receive your BMI value and general category.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, a person weighing 70 kg with a height of 1.75 meters has a BMI of approximately 22.9.

Adult BMI Categories

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obesity: 30.0 or higher

Important Information About BMI

BMI is a screening tool rather than a complete measure of health. It does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately represent body composition in athletes, highly muscular people, older adults, or some other groups.

Other factors such as waist measurement, physical activity, diet, body composition and medical history can also be important when evaluating health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI is a general screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat.

What BMI range is generally considered healthy for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range for adults.
