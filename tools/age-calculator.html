What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that determines a person's chronological age from their date of birth. Instead of manually counting the years, months and days between two dates, you can use SmartToolsKit's free Age Calculator to get an accurate result in seconds.

The calculator can show your exact age in years, months and days. It can also calculate your age on a selected date, making it useful when you need to determine an age for forms, applications, registrations, school records, financial services or personal planning.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  4. Your exact age will be displayed in years, months and days.
  5. The calculator will also show your total years, total months, total days and the date of your next birthday.

How Age Is Calculated

Age is generally calculated by comparing a person's date of birth with a specific calculation date. The difference is determined using calendar years, months and days rather than simply dividing the total number of days by 365.

This approach is useful because calendar years have different lengths and leap years can contain an additional day. The SmartToolsKit Age Calculator accounts for calendar dates when determining the displayed age.

Common Uses of an Age Calculator

  • Checking your current age
  • Calculating age on a specific date
  • Preparing forms and applications
  • Checking eligibility based on age
  • Planning birthdays and important dates
  • Calculating age for educational or professional records
