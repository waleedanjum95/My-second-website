Understanding Meta Tags, Search Engine Optimization, and Social Sharing

In modern web development and digital marketing, meta tags serve as the foundation of on-page search engine optimization (SEO). Placed within the HTML head section of a webpage, these snippets of text provide search engines like Google and Bing with critical context regarding your content, structure, and indexing permissions.
