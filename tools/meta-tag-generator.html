Understanding Meta Tags, Open Graph Protocols, and Search Engine Optimization

In modern web development and digital publishing, search engine optimization (SEO) and social media visibility depend heavily on proper document metadata. When search crawlers or social platforms index a web page, they read the HTML head section to extract titles, descriptions, canonical references, and sharing directives. Without optimized meta tags, web pages often suffer from poor click-through rates and unformatted social cards.
