What Is a Discount Calculator?

A Discount Calculator is a simple online tool that helps you determine how much money you can save when an item is offered at a percentage discount. It also calculates the final price after the discount has been applied.

SmartToolsKit's free Discount Calculator is useful for shopping, budgeting, retail pricing and everyday calculations. Enter the original price and discount percentage to instantly calculate your savings and final sale price.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price of the product.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click Calculate Discount.
  4. View the discount amount and final price.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Suppose an item costs 2,000 and has a 25% discount. The discount amount is 500, so the final price is 1,500. This means the customer saves 500 from the original price.

Why Use a Discount Calculator?

Calculating discounts mentally can be difficult, especially when comparing multiple products or different promotional offers. A discount calculator provides a quick and reliable way to check your actual savings and final price before making a purchase.

How Discount Percentages Work

A percentage discount represents a portion of the original price that is removed from the selling price. For example, a 10% discount means that the customer pays 90% of the original price, while a 50% discount means the customer pays half of the original price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the discount amount calculated?

The discount amount is calculated by multiplying the original price by the discount percentage and dividing the result by 100.

How do I calculate the final sale price?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price to calculate the final sale price.

Is this Discount Calculator free?

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The calculator is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount results in a final price of zero, assuming no additional fees or charges apply.
