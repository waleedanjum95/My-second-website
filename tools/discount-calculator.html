The Mathematics Behind Discounts: How to Calculate Manually

If you ever need to calculate a discount manually on paper or in your head, the formula is straightforward. There are two primary steps involved in determining a markdown price:

Formula 1: Calculate the Savings Amount
Savings = Original Price $\times$ (Discount Percentage / 100)

Formula 2: Calculate the Final Sale Price
Final Price = Original Price - Savings Amount

For example, if a jacket costs \$120 and is on a 25% discount:
1. Savings = $120 \times (25 / 100) = \$30$
2. Final Price = $\$120 - \$30 = \$90$
You save \$30 and pay only \$90 for the jacket!
