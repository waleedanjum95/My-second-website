The Mathematics of Discounts: Manual Formulas

Understanding how percentage discounts work mathematically helps build confidence when shopping or managing retail pricing. There are two primary formulas used to determine markdowns:

Step 1: Calculate the Savings Amount
Savings = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Step 2: Calculate the Final Sale Price
Final Price = Original Price - Savings Amount

Practical Example: Suppose you want to purchase a jacket priced at \$150 with a 20% discount offer.
1. Savings = \$150 × (20 ÷ 100) = \$30.
2. Final Price = \$150 - \$30 = \$120.
You save \$30 and pay only \$120 total!
