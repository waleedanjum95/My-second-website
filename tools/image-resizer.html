The Mathematical Logic of Image Rescaling and Aspect Ratios

Digital image resizing relies on precise geometric scaling formulas and aspect ratio preservation algorithms:

Core Resizing & Scaling Formulas: 1. Aspect Ratio ($AR$):
• $AR = \frac{Width_{original}}{Height_{original}}$

2. Proportional Height Calculation ($H_{new}$):
• $H_{new} = \frac{W_{new}}{AR}$

3. Proportional Width Calculation ($W_{new}$):
• $W_{new} = H_{new} \times AR$

These mathematical models ensure that scaling an image up or down maintains geometric integrity, preventing image stretching or compression distortion.
