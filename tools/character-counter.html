What Is a Character Counter?

A Character Counter is an online text analysis tool that counts the total number of characters in your text. It can also calculate characters without spaces, words, sentences and paragraphs. This makes it useful for writers, students, bloggers, social media users, marketers and anyone working with text that has a specific length requirement.

SmartToolsKit's Character Counter is designed to provide quick results without requiring registration or additional software. Enter or paste your text into the text box and the statistics are calculated instantly.

How to Use the Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text area.
  2. Click the "Count Text" button or simply continue typing.
  3. Review the total character count.
  4. Check the character count without spaces.
  5. Review the word, sentence and paragraph counts.
  6. Use the Clear button when you want to start again.

Characters With and Without Spaces

Character count can be measured in different ways. The total character count includes letters, numbers, punctuation marks and spaces. The "Without Spaces" count excludes whitespace characters, making it useful when a platform or application specifies a character limit that does not count spaces.

Why Character Count Is Important

Many digital platforms use character limits. Social media posts, headlines, advertisements, meta descriptions, form fields and messaging services may have recommended or maximum lengths. Checking character count before publishing can help you keep your content within the required limit.

Character Counter for SEO

Character counting can also be useful when preparing SEO content. Website titles and meta descriptions are commonly written with length guidelines in mind. A character counter makes it easier to review text length before adding content to a website.

Character Counter for Social Media

Social media platforms can impose different text limits depending on the type of content. A character counter provides a convenient way to prepare short-form content and check its length before publishing.
