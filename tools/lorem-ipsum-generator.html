What Is Lorem Ipsum and Where Does It Come From?

Lorem Ipsum is not random nonsense. It is adapted from De finibus bonorum et malorum, a philosophical work written by Cicero in 45 BC. Printers in the 1500s scrambled passages from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 to create type specimens, and the tradition continued through desktop publishing and modern web design. Because the altered Latin has a natural rhythm of short and long words, it resembles real language without distracting readers with meaning.

Tips for Using Dummy Text Responsibly

Placeholder text is meant for development and review only. Always replace it before publishing, because search engines favor original, helpful content and visitors quickly lose trust in pages filled with filler. Search your project for the word "lorem" before launch to confirm nothing was left behind. For accessibility testing, also try real sentences, since screen readers and translation tools behave differently with Latin text.
