Why Error Correction and Redundancy Matter

QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction algorithms to restore damaged or obstructed data. Choosing the right error correction level (ranging from L 7% up to H 30%) determines whether your QR code can still be scanned successfully when partially covered by a brand logo or subjected to wear and tear.

Error Correction Level Recovery Capacity Best Recommended Use Case
Level L (Low)~7% recoveryClean digital screens and high-contrast signage with minimal risk.
Level M (Medium)~15% recoveryStandard business cards, flyers, and general marketing print.
Level Q (Quartile)~25% recoveryOutdoor packaging or high-touch surfaces prone to scratches.
Level H (High)~30% recoveryBranded QR codes requiring central logo placement or heavy graphics.
