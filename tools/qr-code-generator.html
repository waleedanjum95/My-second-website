What Is a QR Code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website URLs, text, contact details and other data. Smartphones can scan QR codes quickly using their camera or a compatible QR scanning application.

SmartToolsKit's QR Code Generator provides a simple way to create QR codes from text and website links. You can generate a code directly in your browser without creating an account.

How to Create a QR Code

  • Enter text or a website URL in the text box.
  • Click the "Generate QR Code" button.
  • Your QR code will appear below the input box.
  • Click "Download QR Code" to save the generated image.

What Can You Put in a QR Code?

You can use a QR code for website addresses, plain text, contact information, product information, event details and many other types of text-based information.

QR Codes for Websites

QR codes are commonly used to make website addresses easier to access. Instead of manually typing a long URL on a mobile phone, users can scan the QR code and open the corresponding website.

QR Codes for Business

Businesses can use QR codes on printed materials, menus, brochures, packaging, posters and promotional materials. A QR code can provide a quick connection between offline materials and online information.
