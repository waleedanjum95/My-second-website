Understanding GPS Coordinates DMS to Decimal Converter

Working with geographic data often means switching between different coordinate formats. While mapping software and GPS receivers typically require decimal degrees (DD), historical maps, legal property deeds, and aviation logs frequently use degrees, minutes, and seconds (DMS). The Math Behind Coordinate Conversions

Geographic coordinates divide a sphere into angular measurements. Each degree contains 60 minutes, and each minute contains 60 seconds.
