What Is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a particular word or phrase appears in a piece of content compared with the total number of words. It can help content creators understand how frequently specific terms are being used.

How to Use This Keyword Density Checker

Paste your article or webpage content into the text box and click Check Keyword Density. The tool analyzes the text and displays total words, unique words, character count, sentence count and the most frequently used keywords.

Why Check Keyword Density?

Keyword analysis can help identify repeated terms, understand the main topics covered by a page and find words that may be appearing more frequently than intended.

Does Higher Keyword Density Mean Better SEO?

Not necessarily. Search engines consider many signals when understanding content. Content should be natural, useful and written for readers rather than created by repeatedly inserting keywords.
