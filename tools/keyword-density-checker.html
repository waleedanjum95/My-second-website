Understanding Keyword Density, Search Relevance, and SEO Best Practices

In modern digital marketing and search engine optimization (SEO), maintaining an optimal balance of keyword frequency is critical for achieving high rankings on Google and other search engines. Keyword density measures how often a specific term or phrase appears relative to the total word count of a document. When managed correctly, it signals topical authority to search crawlers without diluting the readability of your content.

The Mathematical Keyword Density & TF-IDF Formulas
