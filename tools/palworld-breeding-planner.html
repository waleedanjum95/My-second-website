Mastering Palworld Breeding Mechanics

Breeding in Palworld is one of the most rewarding systems in the game. It allows players to bypass difficult wild encounters, hatch legendary Pals early, and breed ultimate combat or working companions equipped with passive skills like Swift, Runner, and Musclehead.

Every Pal in Palworld possesses a hidden numeric Breeding Power value. When you place two different Pals into a Breeding Farm with a Cake, the game averages their breeding power values and searches its database for the Pal whose breeding power is closest to that average.

How Passive Skill Inheritance Works

Offspring in Palworld inherit passive skills from both parents as well as having a chance to mutate new ones. If both parents possess desirable traits like Legend or Swift, the chance of passing them down increases significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Palworld breeding calculation work?

Palworld calculates breeding children by averaging the hidden breeding power values of Parent A and Parent B, then finding the closest matching Pal in the database.

2. Can passive skills be passed down to offspring?

Yes, offspring have a high chance of inheriting passive skills (like Swift, Runner, or Musclehead) directly from either parent.
