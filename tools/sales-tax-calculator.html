The Mathematical Sales Tax Formulas

To calculate sales tax additions and compound totals manually or programmatically, utilize these core equations:

Core Sales Tax & Compound Formulas: 1. Primary Tax Amount: • Primary Tax = Subtotal * (Primary Rate / 100) 2. Secondary / Compound Tax Amount: • Secondary Tax = (Subtotal + Primary Tax) * (Secondary Rate / 100) 3. Total Combined Tax: • Total Tax = Primary Tax + Secondary Tax 4. Gross Checkout Total: • Gross Total = Subtotal + Total Tax

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How is retail sales tax calculated on purchases?

Retail sales tax is computed by multiplying the item subtotal by the applicable tax rate percentage, then adding it to the base price for the gross checkout total.

2. What is a cascading or compound sales tax?

Cascading tax occurs when a secondary tax or local surcharge is applied on top of the primary tax-inclusive subtotal, resulting in compound taxation.

3. Are my financial calculations saved on external servers?

No. All computations execute securely inside your local web browser client-side, ensuring complete data privacy.
