What Is a HEIC to JPG Converter?

A HEIC to JPG converter changes images stored in the HEIC or HEIF format into the widely supported JPG format. HEIC is commonly used by modern smartphones, while JPG has much broader compatibility across websites, computers and applications.

How to Convert HEIC to JPG

Choose a HEIC or HEIF image from your device and click the Convert to JPG button. The image is processed in your browser and can then be previewed and downloaded as a JPG file.

Why Convert HEIC to JPG?

  • Open HEIC images on devices that do not support HEIC.
  • Prepare smartphone photos for websites and applications.
  • Create JPG versions for easier sharing.
  • Use a highly compatible image format.

HEIC vs JPG

HEIC can provide efficient image storage while maintaining good image quality. JPG is an older and widely supported format that is compatible with most browsers, operating systems, websites and image applications.
