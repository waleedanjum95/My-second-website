The Mathematical Text Reduction and Cleaning Formulas

2. Whitespace Normalization (Regex Collapse):

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is case-insensitive deduplication?

It ensures that words with different capitalizations (such as "Apple" and "apple") are recognized as identical duplicates and merged into a single entry.

2. Can I filter out rows containing specific unwanted words?

Yes. Simply type your unwanted term into the Keyword Filter / Blacklist box, and the tool will automatically strip any matching rows.

3. Is my raw text or sensitive data stored on external servers?

No. All text parsing runs strictly inside your web browser client-side, ensuring complete data confidentiality and user privacy.
