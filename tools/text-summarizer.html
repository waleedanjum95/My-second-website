Why Extractive Text Summarization Matters for Productivity

Extractive summarization relies on objective mathematical algorithms to rank sentences by relevance. By calculating word frequency distributions across the document, the tool identifies which sentences carry the highest informational density, making it ideal for screening long files or drafting executive briefings.

Furthermore, running the workflow locally guarantees zero network latency while delivering instant metrics on information density and compression ratios.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

2. How does extractive summarization work?

It ranks sentences based on keyword frequency and statistical scoring to extract the most informative sentences directly from the text.

3. What is lexical diversity?

It measures the variety of unique vocabulary used in your summary relative to total word count.
