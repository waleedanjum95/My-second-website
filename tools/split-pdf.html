What Is a PDF Splitter?

A PDF splitter extracts selected pages from a PDF document and creates a new PDF containing only those pages.

How to Split a PDF

Choose a PDF file, enter the page numbers you want to extract, and click Split PDF. You can select individual pages or page ranges.

Page Range Examples

  • 1 — extracts page 1.
  • 1,3,5 — extracts pages 1, 3 and 5.
  • 2-6 — extracts pages 2 through 6.
  • 1,4-7,10 — extracts page 1, pages 4 through 7 and page 10.

Why Split PDF Files?

  • Extract specific pages from large documents.
  • Share only the pages you need.
  • Create smaller PDF documents.
  • Separate selected sections from a report.
  • Organize documents more efficiently.
