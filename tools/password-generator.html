Why Password Length and Complexity Matter

Brute-force cracking time increases exponentially with every additional character. While an 8-character password can be compromised in minutes, a 16-character complex password requires centuries of computational effort to crack.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is Shannon Bit Entropy?

Bit entropy measures the unpredictability and randomness of a password, reflecting how many binary guesses are mathematically required to crack it.

2. Are my generated passwords sent or saved on an external server?

No. All password generation happens entirely inside your browser client-side, ensuring complete confidentiality.

3. Why are symbols important in passwords?

Including special symbols drastically expands the character pool size, which exponentially increases the combinations needed for brute-force attacks.
