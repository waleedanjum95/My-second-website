Understanding As the Crow Flies Distance Calculator

When planning travel, plotting aviation routes, or studying geography, knowing the absolute shortest distance between two points on Earth is essential. The phrase "as the crow flies" refers to the direct, straight-line distance across the Earth's surface, ignoring obstacles, winding roads, mountains, and oceans. Unlike driving or flight path distances which account for infrastructure and air traffic corridors, straight-line distance gives the pure geometric separation between coordinates.

The Math Behind Great-Circle Distance

Because the Earth is an oblate spheroid, calculating straight-line distances requires spherical trigonometry. I programmed this tool using the Haversine formula, which determines the great-circle distance between two points given their longitudes and latitudes.
