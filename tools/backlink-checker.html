What Is a Backlink?

A backlink is a hyperlink on one website that points to a page on another website. Backlinks are commonly used as one part of SEO analysis and website promotion.

What Does This Backlink Checker Do?

This tool analyzes a list of backlink URLs that you provide. It checks whether each entry is a properly formatted HTTP or HTTPS URL, extracts its domain and identifies the protocol being used.

Why Analyze Backlinks?

Backlink analysis can help website owners organize referring URLs, identify unique referring domains and review the URLs included in an SEO campaign or backlink report.

Can This Tool Discover Every Backlink on the Internet?

No. A browser-only tool cannot independently crawl the entire web or access private backlink databases. Comprehensive backlink discovery normally requires a search-engine index or a specialized SEO backlink data provider.

Why Does Backlink Quality Matter?

Backlinks can differ substantially in relevance, context, authority, placement and other characteristics. A larger number of backlinks does not automatically mean better SEO performance.
