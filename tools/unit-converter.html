The Mathematical Conversion and Scaling Formulas

Standard Linear & Mass Scaling Formula: 1. Converted Value = Input Value * (Source Unit Base Factor / Target Unit Base Factor) Temperature Conversion Formulas: 1. Common Mistakes to Avoid

A common mistake is confusing imperial gallons with US liquid gallons, or mixing up absolute temperature scales (Kelvin) with relative scales (Celsius and Fahrenheit) during calculations.
