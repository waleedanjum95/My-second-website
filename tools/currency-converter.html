What Is a Currency Converter?

A currency converter helps you calculate the value of one currency in another currency. It is useful for travel, online shopping, international payments, business and financial planning.

How to Use the Currency Converter

Enter the amount, select the currency you are converting from, select the target currency and click Convert Currency. The tool retrieves the latest available exchange-rate data and calculates the conversion.

Supported Currencies

SmartToolsKit includes popular currencies such as USD, PKR, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, INR, CAD, AUD, JPY, CNY and several other major currencies.

Are Exchange Rates Real-Time?

Exchange rates shown by this tool are the latest available rates provided by the exchange-rate data service. Rates can change in financial markets, so the displayed conversion should be treated as an indicative calculation rather than a guaranteed bank, card or cash-exchange rate.

Is This Currency Converter Free?

Yes. SmartToolsKit provides this currency converter free of charge and no account or signup is required.

Privacy
