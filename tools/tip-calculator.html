What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly calculate the gratuity amount on a restaurant, hotel, taxi or other service bill. It can also divide the bill and tip equally among multiple people.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

Enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. Then click Calculate Tip to see the tip amount, total bill and amount per person.

Common Tip Percentages

  • 10% — a basic tip.
  • 15% — a moderate tip.
  • 20% — a commonly used tip percentage in some countries.
  • 25% or more — a higher gratuity.

Can I Split the Bill?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator will show both the tip per person and the total amount each person would pay.
