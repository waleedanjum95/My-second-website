Understanding Date-Based Loan EMI & Amortization

Calculating Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) using exact Start and End dates provides real-world calendar precision. Instead of entering generic tenure in years, selecting start and maturity dates automatically accounts for calendar durations and maps exact due dates for every monthly payment.

An amortization schedule provides a transparent breakdown showing how each monthly payment is split between reducing the remaining debt principal and clearing accrued interest charges. In the early stages of a loan, a larger proportion pays off interest, while later payments accelerate principal reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is loan tenure calculated from Start Date and End Date?

The total repayment months are calculated by measuring the exact calendar month difference between your chosen loan start date and maturity end date.

How do I download the monthly EMI schedule?

Once your EMI schedule is calculated, click the "Download Schedule (CSV)" button to export your complete month-by-month repayment chart into a clean spreadsheet file compatible with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
