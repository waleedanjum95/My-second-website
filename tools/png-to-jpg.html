What Is a PNG to JPG Converter?

A PNG to JPG converter changes a PNG image into the widely supported JPG image format. JPG is commonly used for photographs, websites, social media and situations where a smaller image file is preferred.

How to Convert PNG to JPG

Choose a PNG image from your device and click the Convert to JPG button. The conversion happens directly in your browser. After the conversion is complete, preview the image and download the JPG file.

PNG vs JPG

PNG uses lossless compression and can support transparent backgrounds. JPG uses lossy compression and is generally more suitable for photographs and smaller file sizes.

Why Convert PNG to JPG?

  • Reduce image file size for many photographs.
  • Use a format supported by most websites and devices.
  • Prepare images for platforms that require JPG format.
  • Create JPG copies of PNG images quickly.
