What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator is a handy digital utility designed to solve mathematical problems involving proportions, ratios, and fractions expressed per hundred. Whether you are calculating discounts on shopping, academic grades, financial interest rates, or business profit margins, this tool delivers instant and accurate results without manual math errors.

How to Use the Percentage Calculator

Using our percentage calculator is straightforward. Select your required calculation type from the dropdown menu, input your numbers into the respective fields, and click the calculate button. The tool instantly updates to show your precise answer.

  • What is X% of Y?: Finds a specific percentage of a given total number.
  • X is what % of Y?: Calculates what percentage one number is of another.
  • Percentage Increase / Decrease: Measures the percentage change between an initial value and a final value.

Common Formulas for Percentage Calculations

Understanding the underlying formulas helps clarify how percentages work:

  • Basic Percentage: $(X / 100) \times Y$
  • Percentage Proportion: $(X / Y) \times 100$
  • Percentage Change: $[(Final - Initial) / Initial] \times 100$
