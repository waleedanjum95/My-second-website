What Is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the readable part of a web address that identifies a specific page. For example, in a URL such as example.com/seo-tips, the phrase "seo-tips" is the URL slug.

A clean URL slug can make a web address easier for users to understand and can help search engines interpret the subject of a page.

What Makes a Good URL Slug?

A useful URL slug should normally be short, descriptive and easy to read. Words are commonly separated with hyphens rather than spaces. Unnecessary punctuation and complicated characters should generally be avoided.

Why Are Clean URLs Useful?

Clean URLs can help visitors understand what a page is about before opening it. They can also make links easier to share, read and remember.

Slug Generator for SEO

SEO-friendly slugs can provide useful context about a page's content. Including relevant words in a concise URL can make the structure of a website easier for both users and search engines to understand.

Best Practices for URL Slugs

Good URL slugs are usually short, descriptive and easy to understand. Using relevant words and hyphens can make URLs cleaner and more readable.

  • Keep the slug short and descriptive.
  • Use relevant keywords when appropriate.
  • Separate words with hyphens.
  • Avoid unnecessary punctuation and symbols.
  • Avoid unnecessarily long URLs.
  • Keep your URL structure consistent.
