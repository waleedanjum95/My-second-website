What Is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator creates a scannable code from text, URLs and other information. QR codes make it easy for smartphone users to access digital content without typing long addresses.

SmartToolsKit's free QR Code Generator is simple, fast and works directly in your web browser. No signup or software installation is required.

How to Use the QR Code Generator

  1. Enter your text or website URL.
  2. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  3. Wait for the QR code to appear.
  4. Scan the QR code with your smartphone camera.

QR Codes for Websites

QR codes are commonly used to make website addresses easier to access. Instead of manually typing a long URL on a mobile phone, users can scan the QR code and open the corresponding website.

QR Codes for Business

Businesses can use QR codes to share websites, product information, contact details, menus, social media pages and other digital resources. They provide a simple connection between printed material and online content.

Why Use a QR Code Generator?

Creating QR codes manually is difficult and unnecessary. An online QR Code Generator provides a quick way to create scannable codes whenever you need them. SmartToolsKit makes the process simple and accessible on both mobile devices and computers.
