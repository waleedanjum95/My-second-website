The Mathematical Mortgage Amortization Formulas

To calculate monthly mortgage payments, total interest obligations, and accelerated payoff schedules manually or programmatically, utilize these core equations:

Core Mortgage Formulas: 1. Loan Principal Amount: • Principal (P) = Home Price - Down Payment 2. Monthly Interest Rate: • Monthly Rate (r) = Annual Interest Rate / 12 / 100 3. Total Number of Payments: • Number of Months (n) = Loan Term in Years * 12 4. Monthly Payment (P&I): • M = P * [r(1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1]

Principal Loan Price - Down Payment Price = $350k, Down = $70k $350,000 - $70,000 = $280,000

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How is the monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Monthly mortgage payments are calculated using the principal loan amount, annual interest rate, and total loan term in months via standard amortization formulas.

2. How do extra monthly payments reduce my mortgage term?

Making extra principal payments reduces the remaining loan balance faster, which decreases the total interest charged over time and shortens the overall loan tenure.

3. Are my mortgage calculations saved or tracked externally?

No. All financial computations execute strictly inside your local web browser client-side, ensuring complete data privacy and security.
