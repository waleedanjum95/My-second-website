What Is a Background Remover?

A background remover automatically separates the main subject of an image from its background. The removed area becomes transparent, allowing you to use the subject on websites, graphics, documents and other designs.

How to Remove an Image Background

Choose a JPG, JPEG or PNG image and click Remove Background. The AI model processes the image in your browser and creates a transparent PNG.

What Images Can I Use?

  • JPG images
  • JPEG images
  • PNG images
  • Portraits and people
  • Product photographs
  • Objects and other common subjects

The image processing is performed in your browser. Your selected image is not intentionally uploaded to a SmartToolsKit server by this tool.
