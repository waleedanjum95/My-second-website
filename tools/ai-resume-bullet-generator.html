Why Action Verbs and Quantifiable Metrics Matter in ATS Scanners

Modern hiring pipelines rely heavily on Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) that parse resumes for specific keywords, leadership verbs, and metric indicators. Incorporating strong action verbs (such as "Spearheaded", "Architected", "Optimized") at the beginning of every bullet sets an authoritative and professional tone. Furthermore, backing up career claims with hard numbers (940% revenue growth, 200+ hours saved, 10k active users) validates your contributions and builds instant credibility with hiring committees.
