Understanding Comprehensive Text Analysis and Readability Metrics

In digital publishing, professional copywriting, content marketing, and academic writing, measuring structural text properties is essential for maintaining engagement. Knowing exact word counts, paragraph distribution, and reading duration helps creators optimize their copy for target audiences and platform requirements.

The Mathematical Text Analysis & Complexity Formulas

To compute structural metrics and evaluate readability programmatically, I utilize the following core formulas:

Core Text Analysis Formulas: 1. Reading Time (Minutes) = Total Word Count / 200 (assuming average reading speed of 200 WPM) 2. Average Sentence Length (ASL) = Total Word Count / Total Sentence Count 3. Character Density Ratio = Total Characters With Spaces / Total Word Count
