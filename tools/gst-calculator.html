The Mathematical GST Forward and Reverse Formulas

To calculate GST components, net prices, and gross totals manually or programmatically, utilize the following core mathematical equations:

Core GST & Tax Formulas: 1. Forward GST Calculation (Exclusive to Inclusive): • GST Amount = Net Amount * (GST Rate / 100) • Gross Total = Net Amount + GST Amount 2. Reverse GST Calculation (Inclusive to Exclusive): • Net Amount = Gross Total / (1 + GST Rate / 100) • GST Amount = Gross Total - Net Amount 3. Intra-State Tax Split (CGST & SGST): • CGST = GST Amount / 2 • SGST = GST Amount / 2 4. Net Profit Margin: • Profit = Net Selling Price - Wholesale Cost • Margin % = (Profit / Net Selling Price) * 100

By applying these robust formulas, accountants, retail business owners, and freelancers can verify tax compliance across multiple jurisdictions with absolute precision.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do you calculate GST forward and reverse amounts?

Forward GST adds tax to a net base price, while reverse GST extracts the original base price and tax component from a gross tax-inclusive amount.

2. What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST and SGST are levied on intra-state supplies (split equally), whereas IGST is levied on inter-state transactions and imports.

3. Are my business invoice calculations saved on external servers?

No. All computations run strictly inside your local web browser client-side, ensuring complete data privacy and security.
