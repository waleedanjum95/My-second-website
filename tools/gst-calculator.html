What Is a GST Calculator?

A GST calculator helps you quickly calculate Goods and Services Tax on a product or service. It can calculate the tax amount and the final price after GST is added.

How to Add GST

Select Add GST, enter the original amount and enter the applicable GST percentage. The calculator will show the GST amount and the total amount including GST.

How to Remove GST

Select Remove GST when you already have a GST-inclusive price. The calculator will calculate the amount before GST and show the GST portion included in the total price.

GST Rates

GST rates vary by country, product, service and tax rules. Always use the applicable rate for your specific transaction. This tool allows you to enter any GST rate rather than assuming one universal tax rate.

Yes. SmartToolsKit provides this GST calculator free of charge and no account or signup is required.
