Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is a passphrase?

A passphrase is a sequence of words used to gain security access to a computer system or account, combining memorability with high entropy.

2. Are my custom words sent or stored on an external server?

No. All text processing and entropy audits happen entirely inside your browser client-side, ensuring complete privacy.

3. Why are multi-word passphrases more secure than short passwords?

Multi-word passphrases yield significantly greater character length and word permutation combinations, exponentially increasing brute-force cracking difficulty.

4. How does the live entropy auditor work?

Our client-side engine computes exact Shannon bit entropy and permutation combinations based on active character and word pool sizes in real time.
