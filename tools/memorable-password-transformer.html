To evaluate the strength of transformed passwords objectively, cryptographers utilize information theory and computational complexity formulas:

• $H_{trans} = L_{trans} \times \log_2(R_{pool})$
*(where $L_{trans}$ is output length and $R_{pool}$ is character variation set)*
