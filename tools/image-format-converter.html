The Mathematical Logic of Image Scaling and Compression

Digital image conversion, color space mapping, and data compression rely on rigorous mathematical transformations and pixel matrix formulas:

Core Image Processing & Scaling Formulas: 1. Aspect Ratio Preservation ($AR$):
• $AR = \frac{Width_{original}}{Height_{original}}$
• $NewHeight = \frac{NewWidth}{AR}$

2. Compression Ratio ($CR$):
• $CR = \frac{Size_{uncompressed}}{Size_{compressed}}$

3. Alpha Blending Formula ($C_{final}$):
• $C_{final} = (\alpha \cdot C_{foreground}) + ((1 - α) \cdot C_{background})$

These formulas ensure that resizing, format translation, and background color blending preserve geometric accuracy and visual clarity across all target image types.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Which picture formats are supported by this universal converter?

Our tool supports all major raster formats for upload and converts them into JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF, AVIF, and ICO formats directly within your browser.

2. Why do transparent images turn black in some converters?

Formats like JPEG and BMP do not support alpha transparency channels. When transparent files are converted without background filling, render engines default to black. Our Smart Alpha Background Padder solves this automatically with custom color selection.
